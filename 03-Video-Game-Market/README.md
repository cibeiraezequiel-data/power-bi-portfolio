# Video Game Market Analysis

## Overview

This project presents an analysis of the video game market developed in Power BI using a video game sales dataset.

The dashboard explores global sales, regional performance, platforms, genres, publishers, and individual video game titles.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Video Game Market Overview

The first page provides a general overview of the market through:

- Total global sales
- Number of video games
- Average sales per video game
- Global sales by year
- Global sales by platform
- Global sales by genre
- Regional sales
- Regional sales across the top 10 platforms

### 2. Video Game Analysis

The second page focuses on individual market performance, including:

- Top 10 best-selling video games
- Top 10 publishers by sales
- Top 10 platforms by average sales
- Sales by genre and region
- Number of video games by genre

## Key Insights

- Nintendo leads sales among major publishers, with several of its titles appearing among the best-selling games, suggesting that strong performance is driven by successful individual titles rather than only by release volume.
- Action is the highest-selling genre and also has the largest number of published games, combining strong market presence with a high volume of releases.
- North America is the largest regional market and plays a particularly important role in the performance of leading platforms such as the PlayStation 2.
